# AI Clinic – Admin Guidelines

## Admin Responsibilities
Administrators manage clinic operations, user accounts, departments, and overall system activities.

## User Management
Admins can manage patient, doctor, and staff accounts.
They can review user information and manage access permissions.

## Clinic Operations
Admins can monitor appointments, department activities, billing summaries, and patient service requests.

## Reports
Admins can review operational reports and clinic performance summaries.

## Access Control
Administrative information is restricted to authorized administrators.
Other roles must not access admin-only information.
