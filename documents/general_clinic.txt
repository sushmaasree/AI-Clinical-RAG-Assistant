# AI Clinic – General Information

## About the Clinic
The AI Clinic provides healthcare services, appointment management, patient support, and clinical coordination.

## Appointments
Patients can book appointments with available doctors.
Appointments include the appointment date, time, doctor, department, and appointment status.
Patients should contact clinic staff if they need to reschedule or cancel an appointment.

## Patient Support
Clinic staff help patients with registration, appointment scheduling, billing queries, and general service requests.

## Billing
The clinic provides billing information and supports different payment methods.
Patients can contact the billing department for payment-related questions.

## Medical Safety
This assistant provides general information only.
It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medicines, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.
Patients should consult a doctor for medical concerns.
