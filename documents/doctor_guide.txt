# AI Clinic – Doctor Guidelines

## Doctor Responsibilities
Doctors provide medical consultations, review patient information, and manage clinical records.

## Appointments
Doctors can view their assigned appointments and appointment schedules.

## Clinical Records
Doctors can review relevant patient clinical records and document consultation details.

## Follow-ups
Doctors can review follow-up requirements and provide follow-up instructions to patients.

## Medical Safety
Doctors must use professional medical judgment when making clinical decisions.
AI-generated information is only supportive and must not replace professional judgment.

## Access Control
Clinical information is restricted to authorized doctors and other permitted users.
