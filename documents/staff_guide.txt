# AI Clinic – Staff Guidelines

## Staff Responsibilities
Clinic staff support daily operations, patient registration, appointment scheduling, and service coordination.

## Patient Registration
Staff can help patients register and update their basic profile information.

## Appointment Management
Staff can schedule appointments, check appointment status, and assist with rescheduling or cancellations.

## Patient Requests
Staff can view and respond to permitted patient service requests.

## Billing Support
Staff can assist patients with billing questions and direct complex payment issues to the billing department.

## Access Control
Staff can access operational information required for their duties.
They must not access restricted doctor or administrator information.
