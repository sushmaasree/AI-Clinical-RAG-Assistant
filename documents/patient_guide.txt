# AI Clinic – Patient Guide

## Patient Services
Patients can use the clinic system to manage appointments and access permitted healthcare information.

## Appointments
Patients can book appointments and view their appointment details.
They can contact clinic staff for help with cancellations or rescheduling.

## Medical Records
Patients can view their own permitted medical information through the clinic system.

## Follow-up Support
Patients can check follow-up information and contact the clinic if they have questions.

## Billing
Patients can view permitted billing information and contact the billing department for payment-related queries.

## Medical Safety
This assistant provides general healthcare information only.
It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe medicines.
Always consult a qualified doctor for medical advice.

## Access Control
Patients can access only their own permitted information.
